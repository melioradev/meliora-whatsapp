What this page shows

The round green button at the bottom-right is a WhatsApp chat button. During business hours it is a plain link to https://wa.me/… with a message already written. Outside them it says when you’ll get a reply, and offers to send anyway or to email instead.

It shares the corner with the Meliora accessibility button and stays clear of the Meliora Consent cookie banner. Try a narrow window, or the accessibility menu’s text size.

Install on your site

<link rel="stylesheet" href="/meliora-whatsapp/meliora-whatsapp.css">
<script src="/meliora-whatsapp-config.js"></script>
<script src="/meliora-whatsapp/meliora-whatsapp.js" defer></script>

with /meliora-whatsapp-config.js:

window.MelioraWhatsAppConfig = {
  phone: '972501234567',
  message: {he: 'שלום, יש לי שאלה', en: 'Hello, I have a question'},
  hours: {sun: '09:00-17:00', mon: '09:00-17:00', tue: '09:00-17:00',
          wed: '09:00-17:00', thu: '09:00-17:00', fri: '09:00-13:00'},
  email: 'hello@example.com',
};

All the settings are in the README.

Privacy

The button makes no requests and stores nothing. WhatsApp only hears about the visit if the visitor follows the link.
